Enterprise Capabilities

Engineered for Legal & Law Enforcement Rigor

Protecting critical evidentiary records from intake through court proceedings with mathematical certainty and legal precision.

Case Management

Organise cases with parties, assigned team members, status transitions and a per-case timeline.

Evidence Integrity

A SHA-256 hash is computed at upload. Verify Integrity recomputes it on demand and records each check, flagging any mismatch.

Chain of Custody

Transfers are requested, then accepted or rejected by the recipient. Every decision is kept on a per-item custody timeline with a printable QR label.

Smart Reminders

Email and in-app reminders for upcoming hearings and task deadlines, with overdue tasks highlighted and failed deliveries escalated to the case manager.

Immutable Audit Trail

Significant actions are written to an audit log that is chained by hash, so admins can verify the chain and export filtered records as CSV.

PDF Forensic Reports

Download five PDFs per case: summary, evidence register, custody history, hearing schedule and integrity report.

How it works

From intake to court in four steps

Open a case

Create the case, add parties and assign the investigators who can see it.

Upload evidence

Files are hashed as they arrive and stored outside the public web root.

Hand off custody

Request a transfer, wait for the recipient to accept or reject it, and read the timeline.

Prepare for court

Track hearings and deadlines, then export the case PDFs.
